Upload manual OHLC CSV

For tickers not in Kite's instruments dump (delisted, merged, pre-split shells). The CSV needs a date column and a close column (headers like trade_date/timestamp and adj close/closing also work). Dates can be YYYY-MM-DD, DD-MMM-YYYY, or DD-MM-YYYY. Only instruments you've traded are selectable; ones with 0 price rows are listed first.
